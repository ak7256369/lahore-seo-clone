Content Strategy: The King of SEO

January 25, 2024
Back to Blogs

"Content is King" is a cliché for a reason—it's true. But creating content without a strategy is just noise. You need a plan.

Keyword Research

It starts with knowing what your audience is searching for. Use tools to identify high-intent keywords that are relevant to your business.

Quality over Quantity

Google rewards comprehensive, helpful content. One in-depth, well-researched article is worth ten shallow blog posts. Focus on E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).

Consistency

Regular updates signal to search engines that your site is active. A consistent publishing schedule keeps your audience engaged and returning for more.
