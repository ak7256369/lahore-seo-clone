Preparing for Voice Search Revolution

February 1, 2024
Back to Blogs

With smart speakers and voice assistants becoming ubiquitous, voice search is changing how we optimize content. It's conversational, immediate, and local.

Natural Language

Voice queries are longer and more conversational than typed searches. Instead of "SEO agency Lahore," people ask "What is the best SEO agency in Lahore?" Optimize for these long-tail keywords.

Featured Snippets

Voice assistants often read the answer from the featured snippet (position zero). Structuring your content to answer questions directly increases your chances of being chosen.

Local Intent

Many voice searches have local intent ("near me"). Ensure your Google My Business profile is optimized and your local citations are consistent.
