Mastering Technical SEO

January 20, 2024
Back to Blogs

Technical SEO is the foundation of any successful search strategy. If search engines can't crawl and index your site effectively, your content won't be seen.

Site Speed

Google's Core Web Vitals update made speed a ranking factor. Ensure your images are compressed, your code is minified, and your server response time is low.

Mobile Friendliness

With mobile-first indexing, your mobile site is what Google considers the primary version. Ensure your responsive design is flawless.

Structured Data

Schema markup helps search engines understand the context of your content. Implementing it correctly can lead to rich snippets in search results, increasing your CTR.
